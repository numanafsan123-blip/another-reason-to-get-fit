Legal & Health Notice

Educational & Medical Disclaimer

Please review the following information regarding the scope and limitations of the content, workout tables, and calculators provided on this platform.

1. Not Medical or Clinical Advice

The content on Another Reason to Get Fit—including all written guides, workout split routines, set/rep tables, rest interval recommendations, dietary advice, and mathematical calculator outputs—is published solely for informational and educational purposes. It does not constitute medical, clinical, or formal nutritional advice and should never be treated as a substitute for professional medical consultation, diagnosis, or treatment.

2. Physical Exercise & Resistance Training Risks

Engaging in physical exercise and heavy resistance training inherently involves risk of injury. The workout schedules provided on this site are generic educational templates designed to illustrate programming principles (such as full-body vs. upper/lower splits and rest periods). Before commencing any new physical fitness program, users should consult with a certified medical physician or qualified fitness professional to assess individual physical readiness and cardiovascular health.

3. Nutritional Recommendations & Calculators

The calorie, macronutrient, and body composition calculators on this website use standardized population-level mathematical equations (the Mifflin-St Jeor and US Navy Circumference methods). These formulas provide statistical approximations, not individualized biological guarantees. Individual metabolic rates, gastrointestinal health, hormonal variations, and medical conditions can significantly alter energy expenditure and nutrient partitioning.

4. Academic Context & Analytics Privacy

This website is built as an academic portfolio project for MKT901 – Web Marketing at Lovely Professional University. To satisfy assignment requirements, the website utilizes Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to observe traffic acquisition, user engagement, and event interactions. We do not collect, transmit, or monetize any personally identifiable health data (PII). All calculator variables (height, weight, circumferences) are computed 100% locally in the client’s browser and are discarded immediately upon page refresh.

If you have questions regarding the educational citations or formulas used on this website, please reach out via our Contact Form.
