How This Calculator Works (Formulas & Methodology)

To ensure scientific integrity and academic transparency for MKT901, this calculator uses the peer-reviewed Mifflin-St Jeor Equation, recognized by the American Dietetic Association as the most accurate formula for estimating BMR in healthy adults without specialized laboratory calorimetry equipment:

Variable Men Equation Women Equation
BMR Calculation 10 × wt(kg) + 6.25 × ht(cm) - 5 × age + 5 10 × wt(kg) + 6.25 × ht(cm) - 5 × age - 161
TDEE Calculation TDEE = BMR × Activity Multiplier (1.2 to 1.725)
Deficit / Surplus Fat Loss: TDEE - 400 kcal | Muscle Gain: TDEE + 300 kcal
