Understanding Estimations vs. Clinical Diagnostics

Every mathematical formula used in exercise science yields an approximation. Your metabolic rate adapts dynamically based on sleep, hormone balance, non-exercise physical activity (NEAT), and nutritional adherence. Use these calculators as a structured starting line, observe body weight trends over 2–3 weeks, and adjust gradually.
