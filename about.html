Academic Portfolio & Rationale

About "Another Reason to Get Fit"

A deliberate, research-grounded web marketing project created for Academic Task 1 of MKT901 (Web Marketing). Designed to measure genuine user interest across fitness pathways, rest interval education, and Indian nutritional models.

1. The Academic Purpose (MKT901 CA1)

The objective of this project is to implement, optimize, and analyze a fully functional website using online marketing tools and web analytics. Rather than creating a generic placeholder site, Another Reason to Get Fit was constructed with real user intent in mind, focusing on search engine discoverability, user journey friction reduction, and rigorous Google Analytics 4 (GA4) event measurement.

The project directly targets the course evaluation parameters:

  • Creation of Goals (10 Marks): Defining clear macro-conversions (e.g., calorie calculations, body composition outputs) and micro-conversions (article-to-tool CTA clicks).
  • Generating Necessary Reports (10 Marks): Structuring standard GA4 acquisition, engagement, and funnel explorations.
  • Interpretation of Metrics (10 Marks): Deriving actionable digital marketing insights (e.g., which content topics drive tool adoption).
  • Report Submission & Presentation (20 Marks): Professional documentation, technical auditing, and data visualization.

2. Why Fitness and Diet? (And Why Not Wellness)

Modern online fitness content is saturated with vague, nebulous "wellness" tropes—detoxes, cleanses, spiritual alignment, and expensive branded lifestyle products. Wellness is deliberately excluded from this project's scope.

Instead, the website focuses strictly on two verifiable, actionable domains:

  1. Physical Fitness: The physiological mechanics of progressive overload, neuromuscular adaptation, and the critical importance of rest between sets.
  2. Everyday Indian Nutrition: The thermodynamic reality of calories and macronutrients, translated into realistic Indian dietary patterns (dal, paneer, eggs, soy, curd, rice, and roti) accessible on a student budget.

3. Technical & Architectural Choices

To ensure maximum web performance, instant page speeds, and flawless mobile responsiveness, the entire platform is hand-crafted with:

  • Semantic HTML5: Clean hierarchical structure (`<article>`, `<section>`, `<header>`, `<nav>`) optimized for search engine crawlability.
  • Zero-Dependency CSS3: Custom design system with CSS Grid, Flexbox, and fluid typography. No bulky frameworks like Bootstrap or Tailwind.
  • Vanilla JavaScript: High-performance local scripts for mathematics, validation, and analytics dispatch with zero external dependencies.
  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: All calculator equations (Mifflin-St Jeor and US Navy circumference) run locally in the visitor's browser. No private biometric data is ever stored on servers or transmitted to third parties.

Educational & Non-Clinical Disclaimer

This website is purely informational and educational. It does not provide medical diagnoses, clinical dietetics, or individualized prescriptions. Visitors with underlying health conditions should consult qualified healthcare practitioners before initiating heavy resistance routines or aggressive caloric restrictions.
